Illustrating the four-zone limit. Riverside Arena's canonical demo configuration uses 3 zones, as in the first device; a fourth zone is added here only to show what happens at the limit.

Four zones is the validated limit. Two to four zones only - remove one to add another.
  1. Zone cards state cell counts derived from the grid, not an area typed by hand: 800, 800 and 600 cells for A, B and C, matching the observed, dwell, stale and gap counts already fixed in the canonical dataset for this venue. FR9.6
  2. Cell selection opens over the plan, never a free-text area name. Each zone's thumbnail shows the boundary its cells actually cover, using a plain grey outline - never a risk-band colour - because this is a presentation grouping, not a measurement. FR9.6
  3. Not every cell has to belong to a zone. 200 cells here are unassigned, stated directly rather than left to be inferred from counts not summing to 2400 - a zone is a presentation layer over the grid, and coverage of every cell by some zone is not a requirement. FR9.6
  4. Add zone is enabled and shows its position against the limit - "3 of 4" - so the ceiling is visible before it is hit, not only announced at the moment it blocks. FR6.5
  5. At four zones, Add zone is visibly disabled with the limit stated inline. This is the literal acceptance surface for the scope boundary in CLAUDE.md and srs.md 2.7: two to four zones is validated scope, and a fifth is never offered, not even as a greyed-out promise of "coming soon".
