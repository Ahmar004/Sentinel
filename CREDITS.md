# Image credits

The Sentinel login screen shows photographs of very high crowd density to
establish what the system is for. No other screen carries photography.

Every image is used under an open licence (Creative Commons or public
domain) and is attributed on the login screen and in full here. Images are
bundled in `public/login/` rather than hotlinked, in keeping with the
project's no-external-service rule.
