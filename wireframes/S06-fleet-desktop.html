  1. Nav rail carries only two entries. A Drone Operator reaches the live map and the fleet and nothing else; Timeline, History, Replay, Configuration, Accounts, Audit and Health are absent, not disabled, because the role cannot open them. FR10.3
  2. 4 drones, 3 zones, stated side by side. The mismatch is deliberate (decision D5): one zone gets two drones, one gets one, and the count can never be read as "one drone per zone." The sentence beside the counts closes the gap between what a viewer might assume and what the architecture actually enforces. FR9.8
  3. Each card carries state, dwell, link, battery, area and contribution together. Nothing about a drone's status requires opening a second screen. FR1.2
  4. D-03 contributes "DENSITY ONLY", never a cell count. A transiting drone still covers 44 cells, but writing a number there would claim those cells carry the same kind of measurement as an observing drone's. The card names the reason in words instead. FR2.3
  5. D-04's link reads DEGRADED, not a hidden retry. The fleet screen is where an operator notices a connectivity problem before it becomes a coverage gap on the map. FR6.1
  6. Assign opens D07, visible to this role. Assign is present because FR9.8 gives drone assignment to the Drone Operator as well as the Administrator; nothing else on this card is a decision this role cannot make. FR9.8
  7. The out-of-scope boundary is stated, not implied. This sentence is the same one SRS 2.6 uses for the constraint itself, placed exactly where an examiner would otherwise wonder whether "combined feeds" means stitched video. SRS 2.6
  8. Each small multiple is the wind-map overlay over one drone's own footprint only. D-01's cells carry real risk bands because Zone B is elevated where D-01 is stationed; D-02's are calm, matching Zone A. Density, flow and band all come from the same per-cell records `S02` renders, at a smaller crop. FR1.2 FR3.1
  9. D-03 renders density hatching with no arrow and no outline anywhere in its tile. Not because its cells scored low, but because a moving camera never produces flow or risk at all (FR2.3). Labelling it TRANSIT next to a tile with no bands is what makes the absence read as a rule rather than a rendering gap. FR2.3 FR3.5
  10. Overlap is marked on both tiles and named on tap. C-020-030 through C-022-030 exist as one record each, written by whichever drone observed them; the "TAPPED" card shows what selecting one of the marked cells reveals: both D-02 and D-04, never a choice between them. FR6.1
