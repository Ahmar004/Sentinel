Example C-042-017
  1. Two previews, side by side, prove the grid is reactive. The current 5 m setting produces 60 x 40 = 2400 cells; the comparison panel shows what 10 m would produce, 30 x 20 = 600 cells, with visibly coarser lines. This is the acceptance test for FR9.2 made visible rather than asserted in prose. FR9.2
  2. The right-hand preview updates from the same value the left-hand form edits - there is one source of truth for cell size, never a separate "apply" step that could let the two disagree.
  3. Origin is the south-west corner, offset in metres, matching the canonical dataset (0 m east, 0 m north for Riverside Arena). The origin marker is drawn on the preview so a non-zero offset would be visible, not just numeric. FR9.2
  4. Cell identifiers are shown in their exact form, C-<col>-<row> zero padded to three digits, because every later screen in the product references cells by this identifier and it is decided here. FR9.2
  5. The regenerate-and-detach warning is stated here even though nothing can yet be detached on a first-run wizard, so the same language an administrator will see later on S12 and D11 is introduced once, not invented twice.
