  1. Nav is a two-entry bottom bar, the same two entries the desktop rail shows for this role. A Drone Operator reaches the live map and the fleet only; Timeline, History, Replay, Configuration, Accounts, Audit and Health are absent, not disabled. FR10.3
  2. 4 drones, 3 zones, stated side by side, exactly as on desktop. The honest line below states the reason in words rather than leaving a viewer to notice the mismatch and wonder about it. FR9.8
  3. Each card carries state, dwell, link, battery, area and contribution stacked, not spread across a second screen. On a 390px card everything still fits without opening the per-drone view first. FR1.2
  4. D-03 contributes "DENSITY ONLY", never a cell count on its own. 44 cells covered, but writing a number there would claim the same kind of measurement an observing drone makes. FR2.3
  5. Assign is present because the role can act on it. FR9.8 gives drone assignment to the Drone Operator as well as the Administrator; tapping it opens `D07`. FR9.8
  6. Combined feeds stacks in a single scrolling column on a phone. This is a designed layout, not the desktop's four-wide row shrunk to fit - each small multiple keeps its full 120px map height so the wind-map overlay stays legible at arm's length. FR1.2 FR3.1
  7. The out-of-scope boundary is stated at the top of the tab, not left implied. The same sentence SRS 2.6 uses for the constraint itself, placed where a viewer would otherwise wonder whether "combined feeds" means stitched video. SRS 2.6
  8. D-03's small multiple carries density hatching with no arrow and no outline. Not because its cells scored low, but because a moving camera never produces flow or risk at all. The TRANSIT chip beside a tile with no bands is what makes the absence read as a rule. FR2.3 FR3.5
  9. Overlap is marked on both tiles and named on tap, identically to desktop. C-020-030 through C-022-030 exist as one record each, written by whichever drone observed them. FR6.1
  10. Fleet list and Combined feeds are real tabs, wired with `wireframe.js`. Each device shows one default state so both can be reviewed without clicking, but the tablist in either device switches panels live. FR1.4
