  1. The two devices are the whole point of this screen. Same layout, same panel order, same table columns - only the values change, so the contrast between OBSERVE and TRANSIT is legible at a glance rather than requiring two separate mental models. FR2.3
  2. Neither device is a video player, and both say so beside the header. Where a camera preview might be expected, the interface states plainly that only per-cell summaries travel the live path. FR1.4
  3. The state banner is a sentence, not a colour. D-03's reads exactly "In transit. Density only. Flow and risk are not produced from a moving camera." - the drone-state rule stated in the interface rather than implied by an absent arrow. FR2.3
  4. The mini map is cropped to the drone's own footprint only, drawing the same three channels `S02` draws - density fill, flow arrow, risk outline - at a smaller scale. FR3.1
  5. D-03's crop carries density hatching and nothing else, built without the arrow logic the shared map renderer would otherwise apply to a not-enough-dwell cell, because here the absence must be total: no arrow, no outline, anywhere in the tile. FR2.3 FR3.5
  6. The dwell panel is where a viewer watches the gate, not just reads about it. D-01 shows 48 of 48 cells already past 30 s; D-03 shows every sampled cell frozen at 0 of 30 s until the drone actually holds position. FR4.2
  7. Registration names the mechanism, not just a status word. "Each frame is matched to a saved reference frame so drone drift is never counted as crowd movement" explains why 412 inliers matters for D-01 and why 0 inliers means flow cannot be trusted for D-03. FR2.1
  8. Estimated people is summed over observed cells only, and D-03 states "No estimate", never "0". Zero would claim the footprint is confirmed empty; no estimate states that nothing has been measured yet. FR1.5
  9. The footprint table names the reason in its own Flow column, not just a blank cell - "not produced (transit)" reads correctly even if a viewer never opens the legend. FR1.2
  10. The closing line is identical on both devices. "The live path carries per-cell summaries only. No imagery is transmitted from any drone" - stated once per screen state, regardless of which drone or which flight state is showing. FR1.4
