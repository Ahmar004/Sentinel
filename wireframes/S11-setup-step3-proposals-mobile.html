  3. The tally states the exact split. 6 accepted, 2 rejected, 1 unreviewed, matching the canonical setup-pass result for Riverside Arena, so progress is legible without counting cards.
  4. The active card is the one unreviewed proposal. Its crop highlights the affected cells on the plan in the blue affordance colour, never a risk-band colour, because this is a proposed attribute, not observed risk. FR9.3
  5. Accept and Reject are per proposal and reversible. Nothing here is final until Save on the Review step, stated directly under the card so a correction later in Step 4 is expected, not a surprise. FR9.3
  6. Continue works even mid-stack. The primary action is not blocked on reviewing every card, consistent with the skip note above - a step this optional cannot also be a gate.
  7. "View all as a list" is the overview the stack deliberately hides. The second device is that list: all 9 rows with the same three outcomes, so a reviewer can jump straight to the one unreviewed row instead of swiping through eight decided cards to reach it. FR9.3
