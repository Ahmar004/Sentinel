  1. Every zone reads 0.70 risk and 4.0 /m2 density, and every field carries a DEFAULT badge. The badge is the honesty distinction this step exists to draw: these values have not been chosen for Riverside Arena, they are what the system ships with, and an administrator who edits a field loses the badge on that field only.
