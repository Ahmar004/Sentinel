  1. Device A: three zones, matching the canonical dataset - A Concourse 800 cells, B North Gate 800, C Arena Floor 600. Add zone is enabled because the demo venue sits inside the validated two-to-four range with room for one more. FR9.6
  2. Zone swatches are grey patterns scoped to this screen (z-a through z-d), not risk-band colours, because a setup screen shows grouping, never observed risk.
  3. Device B: a fourth zone, D West Concourse, carved from the 200 previously unzoned cells. Zones A, B and C keep their exact canonical cell counts, so adding a demonstration fourth zone never contradicts the dataset the rest of this set transcribes. FR9.6
  4. Add zone is visibly disabled at four, with the validated limit stated inline, not just greyed out with no explanation - the sentence names the number and states the boundary holds everywhere in the product, not only on this screen. FR6.5
  5. Every cell belongs to at most one zone. Zone D's cells come only from the pool that held no zone before; no cell is shown moving from A, B or C, because FR9.6 requires a cell to belong to at most one zone at a time. FR9.6
