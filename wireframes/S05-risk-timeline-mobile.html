Live Timeline Fleet History Replay
  1. The chart is the whole screen, not a shrunk copy of the desktop layout. On a 390px width the period control collapses to a single pill button that opens a sheet, because a full tab row and an expanded resolution sentence do not fit beside each other at this width without crowding the chart itself. FR8.5
  2. The resolution notice rides beside the pill even before the sheet opens. A coordinator glancing at the screen sees "0.1 Hz beyond 1h" without a tap, so nobody reads a 24 hour chart believing it is full-rate. FR8.5
  3. Zone A and Zone B keep the desktop's weight-only distinction. Thin for A, thick for B, both labelled at the line, so the same chart survives greyscale on the phone that is actually carried into the field. NFR5
  4. Four band boundaries at the exact SRS thresholds, 0.00, 0.40, 0.70, 0.85, each still labelled even at this width, because a coordinator reading this line under time pressure should not have to hold the thresholds in memory. FR4.4
  5. Zone risk is the zone's worst observed cell. Zone B's 0.78 today and 0.78 yesterday are each one cell's score standing in for the zone, per FR4.3 and FR4.5, not an average that could hide a single dangerous cell. FR4.3 FR4.5
  6. Zone C has no line, and the chart says why in words next to where the line would be. A flat line at zero would claim the arena floor is calm; an absence paired with a banner is the only honest rendering when zero cells were observed. FR6.4 SRS 2.3
  7. Selecting a point surfaces a card, not a floating overlay. Desktop anchors the popover to the point in place; on a 390px chart that overlay would cover the very line it references, so the selection detail sits below the chart instead. FR8.4
  8. Coverage is charted underneath, per zone, on the same time axis as the risk line. Not one combined bar - three separate stacked areas, so a coordinator can tell which zone's coverage dropped rather than only that coverage dropped somewhere. FR4.6
  9. Zone C's coverage strip is the evidence for its missing line above. Zero percent observed for the whole period is stated as a number here, which is what makes the absent line in the chart a fact rather than a design choice. FR4.6 FR6.4
  10. The period sheet lists all four options as one tap target each, at 44px minimum height, with the current selection shown filled rather than merely bold, because this is the control a coordinator uses one-handed. NFR2
  11. The sheet states the resolution consequence before Apply is pressed. Choosing 24 hours or Custom beyond an hour is explained in the sheet itself, not discovered only after the chart redraws. FR8.5
