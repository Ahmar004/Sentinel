  1. Upload, then set extent, then draw scale - in that order. The extent in metres and the drawn known distance are independent inputs; scale is derived from the drawing, never assumed from the extent alone, because the uploaded image's pixel dimensions carry no metric information on their own. FR9.1
  2. The 50 m line is drawn across the south stand, a distance the site operator can verify on the ground, and its two endpoints are shown as handles so the administrator can see exactly what was measured. FR9.1
  3. Drawn length and computed scale are read-only outputs. An administrator can correct the known distance or redraw the line, but cannot type a scale directly, because a scale not derived from a real measurement is the exact number the acceptance test for FR9.1 is checking against. FR9.1
  4. Ground extent is entered in metres, matching srs.md 2.7: 300 m by 200 m for Riverside Arena. This value feeds Step 2's grid, not the other way around. FR9.2
  5. The scale-source honesty note is stated on the same screen, not buried in help text. Every dependency in Sentinel must be free of paid services, and the map technology choice is exactly the kind of decision an examiner asks about, so it is answered here. NFR8
