Live Timeline Fleet History Replay
  1. The header restates which suggestion this is in full: rank, action and the alert it answers. "Confirm suggestion - rank 1", DIVERT, A-1042 - nothing about which option is about to be confirmed is left for the coordinator to recall from the card they just left. FR7.7
  2. The route is five explicit cell ids in order, not "5 cells". A coordinator confirming a diversion can see exactly where people are being sent, cell by cell, not a count that hides the path. FR7.2
  3. The rationale repeats the exact occupancy numbers from the suggestion card: E2 at 240 of 900, E1 at 780 of 1200. These match the canonical dataset exactly - a confirm dialog that quoted different numbers to the card it opened from would itself be a small honesty violation. FR7.2
  4. All five safeguards render as individually named PASS lines, not one summary badge. A coordinator confirming a route should see which specific checks were run - over-threshold, neighbour push, stale cell, unknown cell, exit capacity - not be asked to trust a single "safe" verdict. FR7.3
  5. The advisory boundary is stated in the dialog's own words, at the moment of decision. "Sentinel does not control barriers, signage or gates. Confirming records your decision and notifies your team" sits directly above the buttons, where a user could otherwise assume the system is about to act on the route it just described. SRS 2.6 FR7.7
  6. Confirm is type="submit" on a real <form onsubmit> that calls event.preventDefault(); Cancel is type="button". Enter submits the form and confirms; Esc cancels. Focus is trapped inside the modal while it is open and returns to the suggestion card's Confirm button once it closes, so a coordinator working by keyboard never loses their place. FR7.7
  7. Mobile keeps every field from the desktop dialog: the target exit, all five route cells, the exact rationale numbers, and all five named safeguards. A decision dialog is exactly where shortening content for width would be indefensible - the sheet scrolls rather than cutting anything a coordinator needs to decide. FR7.1 FR7.3
  8. The mobile sheet does not close on backdrop tap or drag-down, unlike the alert sheet in `S02`. This is the one dialog in the product where an accidental dismissal that silently loses the decision would be worse than requiring an explicit tap on Cancel. FR7.7
  9. The parent screen is kept as a simplified greybox behind the scrim, map and alert rail dimmed, so a reviewer sees the dialog in the context it actually opens from - the rank 1 card in `C05-suggestions` - rather than judging it floating alone. FR7.1
