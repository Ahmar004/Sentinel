COVERAGE UNDERNEATH - same time axis as the chart above
A zone's risk line is meaningless without knowing how much of the zone was seen at that instant, so coverage is charted underneath on the same time axis, never in a separate view. Zone C carries no observed layer at all across the period, which is exactly why it has no risk line in the chart above - not a flat zero, an absence.
  1. Period selector: 15 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, custom. 24 hours is selected here because it is the state that must be reviewed - the resolution notice only exists once a period longer than the full-rate hour is chosen. FR8.5
  2. The resolution notice names the actual sample rate. Beyond the most recent hour, cell history is retained at one sample per 10 s, not 1 Hz, and the chart states this instead of quietly drawing a smoother line than the data supports. FR8.5
  3. Risk lines are grey, distinguished by weight, not by hue. Zone A is thin, Zone B is thick, and both carry a text label at the line, so the chart survives greyscale and colour blindness the same way the map's eight treatments do. NFR5
  4. Four band boundaries, drawn at the exact SRS thresholds. 0.00, 0.40, 0.70 and 0.85, each labelled and coloured with its band's own colour, so a reader sees which band any zone sat in at any moment without a separate legend lookup. FR4.4
  5. Zone risk is the zone's worst cell, already normalised. Each score plotted here is 0 to 1 per FR4.3, and the line is the maximum across the zone's observed cells per FR4.5 - Zone B's 0.78 is one cell's score standing in for the whole zone, not an average. FR4.3 FR4.5
  6. Zone C has no line - not a line pinned at zero. Zero observed cells means no risk can be produced, and drawing a flat line at 0.00 would claim the floor is calm when it is simply unseen. The banner states the reason on the chart itself. SRS 2.3 FR4.6
  7. Alert markers share the risk axis. A-1039 (yesterday, now closed) and A-1042 (current, open) both mark the instant they were raised, so risk history and alert history read off one timeline instead of two. FR5.1
  8. Selecting a point offers "Replay from here." Shown here anchored to the current alert as a worked example: choosing any point on a zone's line opens `S10` scrubbed to that exact timestamp, so an incident is investigated at the resolution it was recorded at. FR8.4
  9. Coverage is charted underneath, per zone, on the same time axis. Not a single combined bar - three separate stacked areas, so a dip in Zone A's coverage is never mistaken for a dip in Zone B's. FR4.6
  10. Zone C's coverage strip is the proof for its missing line above. Zero percent observed for the whole period, the rest split between not-enough-dwell and gap - the same honesty invariant applied to a chart instead of to a single cell. SRS 2.3 FR6.3
