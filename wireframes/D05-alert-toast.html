  1. The toast carries exactly five facts: band, zone, cell, score against threshold, and the single highest contributing feature. "flowConvergence +0.19" is the same number the rail card and `D02` show for A-1042, because the toast is a preview of the alert, not a second measurement of it. FR5.1 FR5.3
  2. No scrim, no focus trap. Unlike `D02` and `D03`, a toast never blocks the surface it is reporting on - the map keeps ticking and stays reachable behind it. Tab reaches View then Acknowledge in order; Esc dismisses the toast without acknowledging and returns focus to wherever it was; Enter activates whichever button has focus. FR5.1
  3. Mobile pins the toast to the top of the screen, full width, below the top bar, rather than a corner, because a handheld display has no spare corner to reserve. FR5.1
  4. CRITICAL never auto-dismisses. No countdown, no progress bar - the coordinator must act on it (View or Acknowledge) or it stays exactly where it is, because a condition serious enough for the escalated critical treatment must not be able to disappear on its own. FR5.1
  5. The critical toast's contributing feature can differ from the elevated one. stopStartPulses here versus flowConvergence in state 1 - the "single highest" is computed per alert, not a fixed field the interface always shows. FR5.2
  6. View selects the corresponding card in the alert rail; Acknowledge sets the alert's status to ACKNOWLEDGED and records who and when. Neither button leaves the map - the whole point of a toast over a page navigation is that investigating an alert costs no context. FR5.3 FR5.6
  7. Muting suppresses the audible tone only. The toast, its content, its persistence rule and the rail card underneath are all unchanged - a TONE OFF chip states plainly why no sound played, so a coordinator who expected to hear something is not left wondering if the alert fired at all. FR5.5
  8. No SMS, no email, no third channel is offered anywhere in this dialog or in the user menu it is configured from. In-app toast plus an optional browser push (`D04`) plus an audible tone is the entire delivery surface FR5.5 defines, and every one of them runs with no paid gateway. Offering a channel the system cannot actually send through would be exactly the kind of number Sentinel cannot justify. FR5.5
  9. One toast per alert. A-1042 produces exactly one toast when it first crosses 0.70, matching FR5.1's acceptance criterion; it is not re-issued while the alert stays open, and the mute setting changes only whether that single toast carries sound. FR5.1
