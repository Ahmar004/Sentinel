  1. The highlighted cell stays visible under the drawer, because this is a drawer, not a modal. A coordinator opening C-031-022 never loses sight of where it sits on the map, or of the alert marker and neighbouring cells around it. FR6.1
  2. Only the OBSERVED state earns three tabs. Current, Features and History all have real content to show for a gated cell; the other three states have nothing behind a Features or History tab that is not either invented or already stated in one line, so they render as a single panel instead of tabs with empty panels behind them. FR4.2 FR4.3
  3. The Features tab lists raw values, not signed contributions. D01 and S04 answer different questions: this tab shows what the nine window features currently measure for this cell (FR4.1), while S04's attribution shows how each one pushed the risk score at the moment an alert fired. Conflating them would make one of the two screens redundant. FR2.4 FR1.3
  4. History plots the cell's own risk series with band reference lines and the alert marker. C03 is the same component S04 and S05 use, so a coordinator reads one chart language everywhere in the product. FR4.3
  5. NOT_ENOUGH_DWELL keeps density and flow, because FR2.3 only requires the drone to be observing, not the window to be complete. What disappears is the score, not the measurements feeding it. FR2.2 FR4.2
  6. The dwell progress bar states the exact fraction, "18 s of 30 s", never a percentage dressed up as a score. A reader could otherwise mistake 60% dwell for a risk value of 0.60. FR4.2
  7. The empty History panel says why it is empty in words, instead of drawing a flat line at zero. A flat line would be indistinguishable from a real, low-risk history - exactly the failure Section 2.3 rules out. FR4.3 SRS 2.3
  8. STALE keeps one number, the density, and nothing else - and that number never appears without its age sitting directly beside it. The age badge reuses the same visual weight as the map's own stale treatment, so the drawer and the map never disagree about how much to trust this value. FR3.4
  9. The last-observed line names a drone and a clock time, not a vague "a while ago". A coordinator deciding whether to trust 1.4 /m2 needs to know it came from D-02 six seconds before now, not from an unspecified earlier moment. FR3.4
  10. GAP keeps exactly one fact: the attribute set at venue setup. Walkable, exit, barrier and obstruction are configuration, not observation, so they survive a total loss of live coverage without contradicting the honesty invariant. FR6.3
  11. The GAP drawer states its four absences explicitly rather than leaving four blank rows. "No density. No flow. No risk. No chart." is the whole of Section 2.3's third consequence, spelled out on the one screen built specifically to prove it. FR6.3 SRS 2.3
